# A test run of the role card prompt, version 1

The prompt (`roleCardPrompt`, shared/roleCardPrompt.ts) for Riverbend, with
the circles Food circle and Land circle and next season's goals Feed everyone
and Grow the orchard, was pasted into an AI, followed by the member's
description in role-card-sample-description.md. The member's answers below are
a test member's. Names in the description were made up for the test.

The AI filled what the description gave (title, what it looks after, what it
can decide, what it does regularly, bring me, not mine to hold, time, the
measures, one seasonal goal) and then asked, one question at a time:

1. AI: Which circle does the kitchen role sit in: the Food circle or the Land circle?
   Member: The Food circle.
2. AI: In one or two sentences, what is the kitchen role for?
   Member: Everyone at a work day eats a good meal, cooked by people who enjoy cooking.
3. AI: Finish the sentence "This role exists while...": what does it serve, and when should it change?
   Member: While we cook together on work days. If the work days stop, it folds into the Food circle.
4. AI: You said Ines handles the food budget money. What is the title of the role she holds?
   Member: Treasurer.
5. AI: What are the first signs the kitchen work has grown too much for whoever holds it?
   Member: Orders going in late, and me cooking every shift myself.
6. AI: What support and feedback come back to you in this role?
   Member: The volunteers tell me how the meal went after each work day, and the farm co-op tells me what's in season.
7. AI: How could you slow down or hand work on, if you needed to?
   Member: Hand a Saturday to another cook, or ask the Food circle to cover the ordering for a week.
8. AI: What will be true at the end of next season because of you?
   Member: The kitchen runs on a rota, so it never rests on one person.
9. AI: When is the cook rota done?
   Member: When six people each cook at least one shift a moon.
10. AI: Which of next season's goals does this serve, Feed everyone or Grow the orchard, and how?
    Member: Feed everyone. Every work day has a meal for everyone who comes.

The AI's last reply, word for word, is role-card-sample-ai-reply.md. It holds
no name: the member's own is left out, and Ines became the Treasurer.
