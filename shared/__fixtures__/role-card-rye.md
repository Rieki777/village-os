## Role title
Human Operations & Onboarding Lead Steward

### Circle
General Coordinating Circle

### Purpose
Everyone who comes to Amora finds a clear way in and a role that fits.

### This role exists while
This role exists while people are still arriving and need a clear way in. Once onboarding runs on its own through the guide and the runbooks, this role folds into the others.

### What I look after
The onboarding process: how a newcomer is welcomed, invited and helped into a role. The circle decides who joins.

### What I can decide on my own
- Manage applicant onboarding, intake workflows and role invitations.
- Front operations questions in investor and partner discussions.

### Bring me
- Someone who wants to join or help.
- A role that needs a person.
- A team member stuck on how we decide.

### What you can count on me for
- The applicant pipeline, role invitations and community intake, designed and run.
- Coaching circle members on sociocratic process and governance.
- Operational facilitation, and fronting operations questions on team, investor and partner calls and community inquiries.
- Matching applicants and members to roles that fit.

### What I do regularly
- Answer role applications within 2 business days.
- Run onboarding calls with newcomers.
- Front operations questions on team, investor and partner calls.

### How we'll know it's working
- New members active within 7 days.
- Role applications answered within 2 business days.
- Season plans filed.

### Works closely with
- The founder: first fit by feel, then hand-off.
- The Operations role: the intake agent and runbooks.

### Signs I'm stretched
Applications waiting longer than 2 business days. Newcomers going quiet after their first week.

### What flows back
A first fit by feel from the founder before hand-off. Runbooks and the intake agent from the Operations role.

### How I can step back
Name it at the Friday check-in. Onboarding calls pause before anything else does.

### Not mine to hold
Hiring or onboarding anyone without a consent process, binding legal or financial commitments, and holding bank or escrow credentials

### Time
Part of a full-time season

## Role title
Organizational Design & Governance Facilitator

### Circle
General Coordinating Circle

### Purpose
Amora's structure is written down, consented to and held by more than one person.

### This role exists while
Amora's structure is still being written down and taught. Once every circle keeps its own charter by consent, this role becomes a light review.

### What I look after
The shape of circle charters and role cards put forward for consent, and how circle syncs run.

### What I can decide on my own
- Draft and propose governance documents, role cards and circle charters for team consent.
- Facilitate circle syncs and coach team members on self-organisation.
- Record role changes and publish chart changes after the circle consents, shared with the Operations role and the founder's role.
- Act alone only to fix an error, and bring it to the circle at its next meeting. Never approve its own holder.

### Bring me
- A tension about who decides what.
- A new role or circle someone wants.
- A role card that needs writing or reviewing.

### What you can count on me for
- Co-designing governance structures, circle charters and role cards with the Operations role and the founder.
- Facilitating the Monday full team meeting, alternating with the Operations role.
- Facilitation and meeting tools: agendas, consent rounds and decision records the team uses in every call.

### What I do regularly
- Facilitate the Monday full team meeting, alternating with the Operations role.
- Review role and circle drafts within 2 business days.
- Coach circle members on consent decision making.

### How we'll know it's working
- Roles with a full card and a term.
- Chart drafts reviewed within 2 business days.
- Gatherings held on time, decisions recorded.

### Signs I'm stretched
Drafts waiting longer than 2 business days. Meetings running without an agenda or recorded decisions.

### What flows back
Co-facilitation from the Operations role. Consent from the team on every draft.

### How I can step back
Hand a facilitation to the Operations role. Name it at the Friday check-in.

### Not mine to hold
- Investor relationships (the founder's role)
- books, tax, legal advice and filings (professionals)

### Time
Part of a full-time season

## My season

### My aim this season
Every person who arrives at Amora finds a clear way in and a role that fits, every human role has a ratified card, and the structure is held by more than one person.

### My seasonal goal
- Ready to grow the team to 30
  Done when: Onboarding, memory, roles and governance can take in 30 people: a clear way in for each newcomer, team memory they can find and add to, every role with a card and a holder or an open invitation, and decisions made and recorded in Amora.

### Village goals it serves
- Clear organisation and governance structures
  How it helps: every role written down, consented to and reviewed on time.
- A thriving community: potlucks, calls and land tours
  How it helps: newcomers become active members within a week.
- Investor ready
  How it helps: operations questions answered on investor calls, and the org chart ready to show.
