Riverbend role card v1

## Role title
Community Kitchen Coordinator

### Circle
Food circle

### Purpose
Everyone at a work day eats a good meal, cooked by people who enjoy cooking.

### This role exists while
we cook together on work days. If the work days stop, it folds into the Food circle.

### What I look after
The community kitchen: its menus, its food orders and its volunteer cooks.

### What I can decide on my own
- What goes on the menu.
- Who cooks each shift.

### What you can count on me for
- A menu planned for every Saturday work day.
- Food ordered from the farm co-op on time.
- Volunteer cooks coordinated for every shift.

### What I do regularly
- Plan the menu for each Saturday work day.
- Order food from the farm co-op every Thursday.
- Send the volunteer cooks their shifts.

### Bring me
- Food allergies.
- Recipes.
- Wanting to cook.

### Not mine to hold
- The food budget and its money (the Treasurer).

### Signs I'm stretched
Orders going in late, and the holder cooking every shift alone.

### What flows back
The volunteers say how the meal went after each work day, and the farm co-op says what is in season.

### How I can step back
Hand a Saturday to another cook, or ask the Food circle to cover the ordering for a week.

### Time
About 10 hours a week

## How we'll know it's working
- Nobody goes hungry on a work day.
- Volunteer cooks come back for another shift.

## My season

### My aim this season
The kitchen runs on a rota, so it never rests on one person.

### My seasonal goals
- A cook rota with 6 people
  Done when: six people each cook at least one shift a moon.

### Village goals I serve
- Feed everyone
  How it helps: every work day has a meal for everyone who comes.
