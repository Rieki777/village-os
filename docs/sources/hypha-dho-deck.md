Roles & Circles
Roles & Circles belong to the DHO, not a person or team.
They are both defined by:
Purpose - Why it exists
Circle - Where it exists
Accountability - What can be expected of it
Domain - What it exclusively controls
Assignment - Person(s) assigned to the role or Lamp Lighting the Circle (can be empty).
The role is not the soul!

Requesting Compensation
How to request value for assignments, contributions or quests. 1. Login 2. Click the + tab on apply for assignment or contribution.
Description Requirements: Here you share your "OKR's" and share the value you're providing the community: 1. Why the org. needs what you're offering. 2. Why you're the person to do it. 3. What can the community expect from you. This applies to role assignments: share what you accomplished the last assignment (major landmarks and value added) and what you intend to do the next assignment. This description is how the community is evaluating whether they will vote for your proposal.
Payout: Put the USD value of your contribution here in the left bar and what % you want to take in Hypha tokens (versus HUSD tokens). For assignments put the % of full time you're filling the role.
Compensation Detailed: These fields will auto-fill based on your USD claimed and % in Hypha selected above. This shows how much HVoice (voting tokens) you're earning as well! Use the tab on the bottom right if you're wanting to claim a custom amount.
Contribution Dates: Select what time period you're planning on doing this contribution or assignment during.

Vote Scope
Each vote is scoped by the Circle/treasury it affects.
Every member holding Voice for in a Circle may partake in the vote. Each Circle may have their own governance.
Local changes: Circles might change their internal voting patterns but can't locally overrule other Circles/treasuries.
"How should we serve our Circle purpose?" / "We use Consent in this Circle." / "We use 1 Person 1 Vote in this Circle." / "We use 1 Token 1 Vote in this Circle."

---
What Requires a Vote?
Voting using VOICE is only required when:
Distributing Org resources; For Contributions, Quests, Assignments and budgets.
Changing the Org governance; For adding/editing/removing of a Role, Circle, Domain, Policy and badge.
Changing the Org Game Guide; To change any of the rules laid out in the Hypha Game Guide.
Changing the Org Smart Contracts; To change any of the Smart Contracts that govern Hypha.
Structural Votes (The DHO Owns): [Circle] Creating a new Circle with a treasury/domain; [Role] Creating roles people can apply to; [Badge] Creating badges people can apply for; [Policy/Protocol] Any policy and/or protocol addition
Activity Based Votes (The Member Owns): [Assignment] All role or badge assignments; [Quest] Any results-based quests for creating, milestones, and finishing; [Expense] Any reimbursement of expenses; [Investment] Financial or other capital investments

Basic DHO Comms
Each Circle by default introduce recurring meetings where each of the following tensions can be processed into meaningful change in pursuit of purpose. The Circle may choose to adopt a policy to change this through their current governance process. Each meeting is to be facilitated by an elected Facilitator and scheduled & administered by an elected Scribe. All role fillers of the Circle and all Links of the Sub-Circles are invited to these meetings.
D Comms (Decentralized) Focus: Governance System. These gatherings are to propose changes to the current governance of the Circle (roles, sub-Circles, policies). The goal is to upgrade the governance system, to make it more joyful, clear, rewarding, empowering, and engaging to create within.
H Comms (Human) Focus: Human System. These gatherings focus on the personal & interpersonal dynamics in the group of people. The goal is to increase the capacity of the group to integrate differences it holds. The goal is to have fun, enjoy each other, build solidarity, and cohesion.
O Comms (Organisation) Focus: Operational System. These gatherings are to inform all roles & members of all relevant operational updates and process any tensions that require attention. Any tension that requires an outcome will result in a project or a next action assigned to the relevant role. The goal is to get s*** done!
