# The Copy Style Key - the enchant-first register (R46)

Ruling R46, founder verbatim: "Lets try a blend with a beautiful and enchanting
way to explain first, with a simple explanation with tooltips throughout (so
one card may have 3 tooltips explaining 3 different parts of the module/etc)."

So the blend inverts from COPY-1: the SURFACE line enchants, in the register
of the founder's own writing, and the PLAIN one-sentence mechanics move into
tooltips. Sources, as ruled: joinseeds.earth, the second brain
(`regen-civics-clean/second-brain/`, chiefly `90 Voice Profile/` and
`08 Concepts/`), and regencivics.earth. Every rewrite in the Copy Book cites
one of the exemplars below by number.

## Exemplars, verbatim from the corpora

1. "What if healing ourselves and our Earth is actually a fun and Infinite
   Game?" - Voice Profile (the framing question, the spine of the project)
2. "Gratitude is a new way of distributing resources in this economy, through
   people just being grateful to each other for the contributions we bring."
   - Concepts/Gratitude
3. "The same gesture recognizes a contribution and moves the collective
   timeline." - Concepts/Gratitude
4. "needs woven together from the whole network into the center, the way
   mycelia weave a forest's roots into one web" - Concepts/Mycelia Governance
5. "Everyone here is here to do one of two things, which are really the same:
   heal and evolve." - Concepts/The Great Work
6. "no piece competes with another, each is needed for the picture to
   complete" - Concepts/Puzzle Pieces
7. "SEEDS is owned by no-one and designed for everyone." - joinseeds.earth
8. "Wherever you are, it's the perfect place to begin." - joinseeds.earth
9. "Unlike finite games played to win, infinite games are played to continue
   playing" - regencivics.earth
10. "the quests prepare us and get us started; the land projects push us"
    - Concepts/We Are the Land
11. "set the initial conditions, then hand the keys to the network"
    - Concepts/Network-Owned
12. "shifting our mindsets to see what's right and what we want more of"
    - Concepts/The Highest Timeline

## The sentence shapes he uses

- A short declaration, then the unfolding clause: "X is Y: and here is what
  that means for you." The colon lands the point (exemplars 5, 11).
- The definitional sentence with warmth: "Gratitude is a new way of
  distributing resources" (2), never a lecture.
- The mirrored pair, two halves of one truth: "the quests prepare us and get
  us started; the land projects push us" (10).
- The nature simile doing real explanatory work: "the way mycelia weave a
  forest's roots into one web" (4). The image explains the mechanism.
- Owned-by-everyone constructions: "owned by no-one and designed for
  everyone" (7).
- Direct address that lands where the reader stands: "Wherever you are, it's
  the perfect place to begin" (8).
- Load-bearing concepts capitalized as proper nouns: Game, Quest, Gratitude,
  the Infinite Game.

## Words he loves

co-create, weave, thread, seed, plant, root, grow, steward, gift, flow,
gather, abundance, alive, harvest, tend, bloom, mycelia, organism, timeline,
puzzle piece, the land, healing, evolve.

## Words he never uses (and the gate bans)

delve, tapestry, foster, leverage, vibrant, crucial, transformative,
testament to, beacon, unleash, seamless, robust, comprehensive, empower,
utilize, unlock-as-hype, navigate-as-metaphor. Also banned by the house gate:
em-dashes and en-dashes, contrast framing ("not X but Y", "rather than"),
rhetorical-question openers as filler, passive inspiration.

## The division of labor on every card

- The surface line ENCHANTS: short, warm, in the map's own register, an image
  that carries the mechanism the way exemplar 4 does.
- The tooltip EXPLAINS: one to three plain declarative sentences a first-time
  visitor trusts, no metaphor, the COPY-1 register. Up to three tooltips per
  card where three concepts genuinely live on it. Never a tooltip inside a
  tooltip.

# R47 - the four voices: the game guides the player (2026-10-05)

R47 builds on R46 and changes one thing about it: the order.

The founder asked for every screen to read the way a world-class MMORPG guides
its players, in his words "clear, playful, and direct", "easy to understand,
direct, and in character" (2026-10-02). On the first draft he added the rule
that governs everything below: "tell them only what they need to know to keep
progressing". He approved the model and the plan that follows on 2026-10-05:
"love All your ideas for improving the plan let's get this done".

## What the games do, and why the order changed

These patterns come from the interface text of World of Warcraft, Final
Fantasy XIV, Guild Wars 2, Elder Scrolls Online and Old School RuneScape.
Wherever their string files are published, the patterns were read from those
files directly:

- Every one of them splits its words into layers. A plain objective sits
  always on screen ("Report to Momodi at the Quicksand."). A neutral system
  voice handles alerts. A character gives the why. Optional story comes last.
- Most players skip the story. In a 2024 World of Warcraft poll, 37.7% said
  they read no quest text at all. The games answered by putting everything a
  player needs into the objective line.
- One next step is always marked, and a lock is shown early and phrased as the
  fix ("Requires Level 10").
- Wins are tiny ("Quest Complete!", "Discovered: X"). Errors are short and plain.

R46 put the enchanting line first and the plain line in a tooltip. R47 puts the
next step first and keeps it visible. The enchanting line becomes the Lore
line: one line, and always last. Tooltips stay, and they now only define words
(consent, cycle, Path of Growth).

## The four voices

| Voice | Its job | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trail** | The next step | Imperative, 3 to 10 words, with a counter where there is one. Always visible. | Next: Sign the Love Letter |
| **Signal** | What just happened, or what is blocked and how to clear it | 1 to 3 words for a win. One sentence for a block, and it names the fix. | Quest complete! / Opens at Member. Sign the Love Letter to get there. |
| **Guide** | The welcome, the why, the thanks | Warm, first person, 1 to 3 sentences. Only where the guide can answer back (her chat, the map). | Hi, I'm Maia. Got a Quest the board is missing? |
| **Lore** | The soul of it | One line, optional, always last | Naming what is good is how the village grows more of it. |

The founder's voice lives most fully in the Guide, the Lore and the NAMES.
Trail and Signal are where clarity wins.

## The rules

1. **Only what moves them forward.** Player copy never explains machinery: not
   servers, admins, ledgers, records, or how a thing is checked. Say the outcome
   ("Nothing was charged"), never the mechanism.
2. **One next step, always visible.** The server already computes it
   (`nextActions` in `shared/gameConfig.ts`, served as `me.nextAction`).
3. **Progress is a number.** "2/5", never a sentence.
4. **Every block names the fix.** "Opens at Member. Sign the Love Letter to get
   there."
5. **Every finish names the next step.**
6. **Celebrate in 1 to 3 words, then get out of the way.**
7. **Teach one thing when it is needed, never before.** A hero holds one door
   forward, not a reading list.
8. **One name per thing, and one voice per kind of message.** See the lexicon.
9. **Thanks comes from people.** The games fake it with a letter from a
   character. This village has real Gratitude.

## Three zones keep their own temperature

- **The Gate** (public pages, sign-in, joining): warm, short, one door forward.
- **The Quest Log** (quests, the walk, the map, the profile, empty states): the
  full playful guide voice.
- **The Ledger** (money, rights, the membership agreement, leaving, privacy):
  plain and exact, with no play. "Checkout cancelled. Nothing was charged." is
  the standard.

## Message shapes

| Kind | Shape | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| Quest title | 2 to 6 words, a verb or a name | Tend the food forest |
| Objective | Imperative, names the thing and the place | Tap Buy on the Exchange. |
| Progress | Number over number | 2/5 stops |
| Start or finish | Label, then a name | Quest started / Quest complete! |
| Stage reached | 2 to 4 words, then the next step | You're inside. Guest reached. |
| Requirement | "Opens at" + stage, then the fix | Opens at Member. Sign the Love Letter to get there. |
| Error | What failed, then what to do | This page didn't load. Reload to try again. |
| Empty | What is missing, then how to fill it | No Quests posted yet. Post the first one. |
| Lore | One line, last, optional | Real work, on real land, beside people becoming your people. |

## The lexicon: one name per thing

| Thing | Say | Never |
|---|---|---|
| The work board | the Quest Board, a Quest (capital Q in player copy) | quest board, mission, task |
| Taking a Quest | Take this Quest | Claim, Accept |
| The quest states | In progress, Turned in, Complete, Returned | You're on this quest, Awaiting consent, Completed, Not accepted |
| The membership ladder | the Path of Growth, a stage | Maturity, membership ladder, membership path, level |
| The token room | the Exchange | Tokens |
| Thanks | Gratitude, the heart glyph | Hearts, points, XP, earnings |
| Getting in | Sign in, Create an account | Sign In, Log in, Create Account, Register |
| A module, in player copy | a room | module |
| The founder's tour | Your First Walk (founders and admins only) | Meet your village |
| One of the four ways in (Investor, Village Steward, Resident, Prosperity Creator) | a path ("Walk this path") | a character |
| Who a member plays on their sheet | a character ("Play this character", "Add a character"). Characters fill roles. | a path |
| A unit of community training | a lesson | a module |
| A position on the org chart (the steward role, a founder role, an operations role) | a role, a role holder, "Apply for a role", "take the role back" | a seat |
| Putting someone into a role | seat ("Seat someone", "seated", "unseat") | |
| How many people a role takes | "Holds up to 3", "2 of 3 held", an opening ("1 opening") | seats, open seats |
| The admin screen that gives people powers | Power groups | Game Roles |
| A place at a gathering | a seat ("Save me a seat", "Seat fee") | |

Path, character and lesson are the founder's rulings of 2026-10-09: "path is
the investor, resident, etc. the others should be character and then
characters fill roles", and "Lessons or learning journey's" for training.

Role is the founder's ruling of 2026-10-21: "use the word 'role' instead of
'seat' when talking about the sociocratic style roles". The verb stays, on his
word the same day: "You can use 'seat someone' for that verb." A seat is still
a seat at a gathering. `scripts/check-voice.mjs` enforces the noun (its rule 6,
SEAT_RULE); stored ids such as `org.seat` and `role_seat` keep their spelling,
because members never read them.

Never XP, gold, loot or "level up": Gratitude is thanks, never pay. Growth
words do the job instead: you grow into a stage, and a room opens.

### Naming who holds a role

The role comes first, always. When a signed-in member reads a line that hands
an item to a role (a task, a live session's action, "Held by"), the first
names of the people sitting in that role now follow in parentheses:
"Treasurer (Rick)", "Steward (Rye, Jessica)", "Steward (Ana, Ben, Cy +2)".
A vacant role is the role alone. The founder's ruling of 2026-10-30: "name the
role AND the person (if there is one) so it's always naming the role as
primacy, just the first name of the person in parenthesis".

This amends the ruling of 2026-10-22 ("we never name people instead we name
the role") for live display only, and the rest of that ruling stands:

- The names are read from the roster when the page is drawn
  (`roleWithHolders` in `shared/roleHolderLabel.ts`), so a handover renames
  every line by itself. Written copy, stored notices, emails, role card text
  and machine drafts still name the role and never a person.
- Public pages, shared links and anything sent to a vendor never carry the
  parenthesis. The Saberra bridge sends role ids and code names.
- A role proposal still counts who holds or applies, since its shared link
  can be read by anyone holding it.

## Who reads it, and who speaks

- Write every page for ONE reader: a visitor, a member or a founder. Lines
  meant for founders show only to founders.
- The guide (Maia, or the name the village gives her) speaks only where she can
  answer back: her chat, the map, Propose a Quest. Menus, buttons and alerts use
  the plain system voice, so she is never the one saying "this page didn't
  load". Her default lines are defaults a village rewrites (map ruling D2,
  2026-10-02).
- Platform copy uses world words that fit any land: village, path, Quest,
  season, moon, room. A village's own flavour comes from its settings and its
  brochure pages.
- Flavour can dress up a fact, but it never replaces or invents one.

# R48 - the blend: the founder's picks (2026-10-21)

R48 builds on R47 and changes nothing in it. It sets how warm each kind of
line runs.

On 2026-10-21 the founder compared nine real lines, each written three ways:
A Trail (clear and quiet), B Catalyst (his broadcast warmth) and C
Quest-giver (in-world). He picked line by line, with no one style overall.
Today's line was kept 3 times, A was picked once, B twice and C three times.
Read together, the picks make one voice with four temperatures.

## The four temperatures

1. **Frames stay clear.** Page titles, ledes and emails keep today's line
   where it already works. Change only machinery talk, inside phrases and
   lexicon breaks.
2. **Thanks are short and plain.** "Thanks." and then the outcome: "Thanks.
   You're checked in."
3. **Thresholds are warm.** When something finishes or opens, thank the
   people and say what opens: "Beautiful work, circle." "Finish your lessons
   and this Quest opens to you."
4. **Quiet places speak in-world.** Empty states, private pages and the guide
   speak from inside the world, then give one next step: "The board is quiet.
   Post the first Quest."

The Ledger keeps its own temperature: money, rights, privacy, deleting and
leaving stay plain and exact in every case.

One more rule comes from his own edit to the Module Library line, "one card at
a time" to "one tool at a time": name a thing by what it is, never by the
screen element that shows it.

## The picks

| Line | Picked | The line |
|---|---|---|
| Live sessions header | today's | Hold a working call together: arrive, set the agenda, decide by consent, and leave with every action in someone's hands. |
| Session check-in, after | A | Thanks. You're checked in. |
| Session closed | B | Beautiful work, circle. The session is closed, and the notes are saved for everyone who was here. |
| A Quest you can't take yet | B | Opens at Member. Finish your lessons and this Quest opens to you. |
| Empty Quest Board | C | The board is quiet. Post the first Quest. |
| Journal lede | C | Your journal. A few good questions wait inside, and only you can read your answers. |
| Module Library lede | today's, his edit | Everything this platform can be, one tool at a time. |
| Work With Us email | today's | (unchanged) |
| Maia's voice | C | Speak as the village's guide from inside its world. Name the place and the people, then offer one next step. |

The session-closed line ships as picked. Asked whether "notes" should become
"record" (Notes already names the note entries inside a session), the founder
ruled on 2026-10-22: "leave it as notes". The close confirmation says the
same.

## Applied first

The Sessions pilot, branch `wt/voice-sessions`: the list page, the room, the
library of waiting items and the task board. Every other zone follows once
the founder has seen the pilot live.
